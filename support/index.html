Getting started

  1. Turn on Personal Hotspot on your iPhone and join it from your car's Wi-Fi settings.
  2. Open Real Mirror and allow the VPN configuration when iOS asks (it runs only on your iPhone).
  3. Tap Start, choose Real Mirror and tap Start Broadcast. Keep your phone unlocked.
  4. In your car's browser, enter the address shown in the app. Use only while parked.

Troubleshooting

  • Page doesn't load: make sure your car is on your iPhone's hotspot and the app shows the car connection as On.
  • Black screen: some video apps block mirroring.
  • Choppy picture on older cars: Settings → Car display quality → MCU2.
  • Sound: pair your iPhone with your car over Bluetooth.

Subscription

Settings → Manage Subscription, or iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Use Restore Purchases after reinstalling.
