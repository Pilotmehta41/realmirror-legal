Real Mirror — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

  • We collect no data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
  • VPN. Real Mirror uses a VPN configuration that runs only on your device to connect to your car. It does not route, inspect, log or send your internet traffic anywhere, and there is no remote server.
  • Screen content is shown in your car only while you are mirroring. It is never recorded or stored.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Free-trial counter is stored only on your device.
  • Children. Real Mirror is not intended for children under 13.
